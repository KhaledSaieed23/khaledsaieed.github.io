Privacy Policy

App Name: Nexora Searching and Filtering

Publisher: NEXORA

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

NEXORA is committed to protecting the privacy of users of the Nexora Searching and Filtering add-in for Autodesk Revit. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

2. Data Collection

The Nexora Searching and Filtering add-in does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information. All operations — including element filtering, smart search, rebar search, and linked elements browsing — are executed locally on your machine within the Autodesk Revit environment. No project data, element information, or file contents are ever transmitted outside of Revit.

3. Tools Included

This add-in includes the following tools:

  • Advanced Filter — Filter and isolate Revit elements using advanced multi-criteria selection rules.
  • Smart Search — Search model elements by name, type, family, or any parameter value.
  • Find Reinforcement — Locate specific reinforcement bars by number and navigate directly to them.
  • Linked Elements Browser — Browse, filter, and inspect elements inside loaded Revit Link files.

4. Third-Party Services

This add-in does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

5. Internet Access

The Nexora Searching and Filtering add-in does not require or use an internet connection. No data is sent to external servers.

6. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
