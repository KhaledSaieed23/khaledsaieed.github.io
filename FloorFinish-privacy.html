Privacy Policy

App Name: Floor Finish Creation & Cutting Optimization

Publisher: NEXORA

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

NEXORA is committed to protecting the privacy of users of the Floor Finish Creation & Cutting Optimization add-in for Autodesk Revit. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

2. Data Collection

The Floor Finish Creation & Cutting Optimization add-in does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information. All operations — including room and floor boundary detection, tile grid generation, cutting optimization calculations, filled region creation, and take-off analysis — are executed locally on your machine within the Autodesk Revit environment. No project data, model geometry, element information, or file contents are ever transmitted outside of Revit.

3. Features and Local Processing

All of the following features operate fully offline and locally:

  • Floor finish creation from rooms or existing floor elements
  • Tile grid layout with waste optimization (Sutherland–Hodgman clipping)
  • Cutting analysis, off-cut take-off, and material quantity export
  • Filled region tile visualization and tagging
  • Off-cut stock management and Excel import/export
  • Analysis of existing tile pieces selected in Revit

None of these features require or use an internet connection.

4. Third-Party Services

This add-in does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or data collection services. The optional Excel export functionality uses Microsoft Excel Interop, which runs entirely on your local machine.

5. Internet Access

The Floor Finish Creation & Cutting Optimization add-in does not require or establish any internet connection. No data is sent to external servers at any time.

6. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
